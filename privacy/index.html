Simply Padel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Simply Padel is made by Avzilabs, an independent developer. Contact avzilabs@gmail.com about your data or a deletion request.

Saves and Steam Cloud

Profiles, created players, career progress, match history, achievements, preferences and local player reports are saved on your PC. Local diagnostic logs can contain player names, match details and errors. The game does not automatically send those logs, screenshots or recordings to Avzilabs.

Steam Cloud can copy the game's save files, including your profile and settings, to Valve's servers when enabled for this game in Steam. You control sync in the game's Steam properties. Turning sync off does not delete previously uploaded copies. Ask Valve about deleting cloud data. Take care deleting local saves while sync is enabled, because an older copy may be restored.

Steam features

Steam handles your account, purchases, achievements, friends and invitations. The game uses your Steam ID and display name for these features and sends unlocked achievements and game activity to Steam. Steam's privacy settings control who can see your profile and activity. This is separate from gameplay analytics.

Training scores stay on your PC by default. If you enable Share training scores on Steam in Settings, Privacy and data, future completed drill scores are submitted to Steam leaderboards with your Steam identity. Other players can view leaderboard entries. Turning sharing off stops new submissions; it does not remove scores already submitted. You can still view leaderboards with sharing off.

Online play

Choosing an online feature connects to Unity Gaming Services for authentication, lobbies, matchmaking and relay. Unity assigns a player ID and processes connection information, including your IP address. The normal online match route uses Unity Relay, so peers connect through the relay rather than receiving your IP address from the game.

Lobbies and matches exchange player IDs and names, selected character appearance and equipment, public player-card details, ratings, scores and gameplay state. Your profile-sharing settings determine which optional profile details are shared. Friends careers also exchange the career state needed to play together. Other players can see the information the game displays and can make their own recordings.

These transfers provide the online features you choose. You can play offline modes without joining online matches. Steam itself may still be connected and syncing saves or achievements.

Voice and chat

Voice is push-to-talk by default. Changing to open microphone lets the game transmit while the microphone is active. Audio & voice settings let you mute your microphone or deafen voice chat. Text and voice are sent to players in the session. The game does not attach a voice recording to player reports, and Avzilabs does not run a chat-recording service.

Player reports and moderation

Reporting a player keeps a local report and attempts to send your Unity player ID, the reported player's Steam or Unity ID, your selected reason, the match ID and report time to Unity Cloud Code and Cloud Save. The game also sends player IDs to check voice-chat bans. Avzilabs can access moderation records to handle abuse and appeals. The reported player is not given the reporter's identity.

Reports count within a 14-day window. This does not mean records are automatically deleted after exactly 14 days: old entries are removed when a later report updates the player's record. Ban records and report-rate-limit records can remain stored until cleared. Request deletion or appeal a ban at avzilabs@gmail.com, with your Steam ID or relevant player details. If a record must be retained to address abuse or a dispute, we will explain why.

Analytics, advertising and crash uploads

Simply Padel has no advertising or gameplay analytics service. Optional Unity Analytics, device-statistics collection and automatic crash uploads are disabled in the game. Avzilabs does not receive a background feed of your play sessions, files, microphone recordings or screenshots.

Valve and Unity still process data required for the services you use, including service security and operational records. Their policies describe their handling and retention:

  • Valve privacy policy
  • Unity game-player privacy policy

We do not sell personal data or share it with advertisers. Steam handles payments; the game does not ask for your payment details, real name or email address.

Support and your choices

If you send a bug report, clip or log by email, Steam discussions or Discord, Avzilabs uses it to investigate and respond. Check it before sharing: it may include names or chat. Public posts can be read by others. You can ask Avzilabs to delete material you sent directly; the platform may retain its own copy under its policy.

Email avzilabs@gmail.com to ask what personal data Avzilabs holds about you, request correction or deletion, or object to its use. Include enough information to locate the record, but never passwords or payment details. We may need to verify that the request is yours. Valve and Unity handle requests about their own accounts and services. UK and EU players can also raise concerns with their data protection authority.

Online data is used to provide the service you choose and protect players from abuse. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it.

Changes

We will update the date and describe material changes in release notes. Adding optional analytics or a new use of player data requires an updated notice before collection starts.
