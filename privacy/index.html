Simply Padel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Simply Padel is made by one person, trading as Avzilabs. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, email avzilabs@gmail.com.

What stays on your computer

Your profiles, career progress, settings, key bindings and achievements are saved on your own PC. I can't see them, and they aren't sent anywhere.

What the game uses when you play online

Online matches run through Steam and Unity Gaming Services (Unity Lobby and Unity Relay). To connect you with other players, the game uses:

  • Your Steam ID and Steam display name. These identify you in lobbies, on your player card and to friends you invite.
  • An anonymous Unity player ID. Unity creates this when you go online so the lobby and relay can tell players apart.
  • Your IP address. Unity's relay servers need it to carry match traffic between players. Other players in your match don't get your IP address, because traffic goes through the relay.
  • Match data. This covers your in-match position, shots and score, sent to the other players while the match is running.
  • Chat. Text and voice chat is sent to the players in your match. I don't record or store it. Voice uses push-to-talk by default, so it's only sent while you hold the button. If you switch to open microphone in Settings, it's sent whenever you talk. You can deafen voice chat in Settings.

Unity and Valve process this data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Valve (Steam): https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/

What I don't do

  • I don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • There are no ads and no third-party analytics in the game.
  • I don't collect your real name, email address or payment details. Steam handles purchases.

Bug reports

If you send me a bug report, clip or log file through Steam discussions, Discord or email, I use it only to fix the problem. You can ask me to delete it at any time.

Children

Simply Padel is not aimed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect data from them. Online chat is between players only, and voice chat can be deafened in Settings.

Your rights

You can ask what data relates to you, or ask for it to be deleted, by emailing avzilabs@gmail.com. Most of it lives on your own PC (delete your saves to remove it) or with Unity and Valve, and I'll help you reach them where needed. If you're in the UK or EU, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top and mention it in the game's patch notes.
